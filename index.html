01 • ABOUT DLP

Technology is more than just screens.

DLP brings digital literacy, cybersecurity, AI, creativity, and leadership into fun, hands-on experiences designed for students.

We believe students shouldn't just use technology. They should understand it, question it, create with it, and help others navigate it.

05 / OUR PURPOSE
WHY DLP EXISTS

Technology is everywhere.

Understanding it should be too.

Students interact with technology every day — but using technology and understanding technology are two very different things.

DLP exists to close that gap.

THE PROBLEM
📱

We use it.

Technology is part of everyday student life — from social media and search engines to AI and online learning.

?

But do we understand it?

Students aren't always taught how the technology they rely on actually works, or how to navigate its risks.

→

That's where DLP comes in.

We turn technology from something students simply consume into something they can understand, question, and create with.

PAST PROJECTS
🎮

Hack the Heist

An interactive cybersecurity investigation game where students solve a digital mystery by analyzing evidence, security logs, emails, and files.

💻

Ctrl+Code

A student-led coding initiative designed to introduce younger students to programming through approachable, hands-on activities.

🔐

Cybersecurity Education

Interactive lessons and challenges covering phishing, privacy, passwords, online safety, and the everyday decisions that keep students safer online.
