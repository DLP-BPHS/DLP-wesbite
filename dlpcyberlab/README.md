
# DLP CyberLab

### Making cybersecurity education accessible to everyone.

DLP CyberLab is an open-source educational initiative developed through the Digital Leaders Program (DLP). It provides free, accessible, and engaging cybersecurity and technology education resources for students, educators, and communities.

This source provides cyber education paired with games and activity for everyone to access easily 

Our goal is to make complex technological concepts understandable through interactive learning, practical activities, educational games, and structured curriculum.

---

## 🎯 Our Mission

To empower the next generation of digital leaders by making cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and computer science education accessible, engaging, and practical.

We believe every student should have the opportunity to understand the technology that is shaping their world and that they are becoming increasingly dependent on.

| Category | Description |

## 🕹️ Featured Projects

Explore our growing collection of educational projects:

- **Phishing Detective** – Learn to identify suspicious messages and recognize social engineering.
- **Digital Detective** – Investigate digital clues and develop online safety awareness.
- **Hack the Heist** – Solve cybersecurity challenges through interactive scenarios.

*More projects and resources are currently in development.*

## 🌱 Who Is This For?

DLP CyberLab is designed for:

- Teachers looking for accessible cybersecurity teaching materials.
- Middle and high school students interested in technology.
- Schools and student organizations developing technology programs.
- Community organizations promoting digital literacy.
- Anyone interested in learning about cybersecurity and AI.

NO advanced technical experience is required to get started.

## 🏫 For Educators and Organizations

Interested in using our resources?

You can:
- Incorporate lessons into classroom instruction.
- Use activities in technology clubs and workshops.
- Adapt educational materials for your students.
- Share our resources with your community.

We encourage educators and organizations to explore, adapt, and contribute to the curriculum in accordance with its licensing terms.

## 🤝 Contributing

DLP CyberLab welcomes contributions from students, educators, developers, and cybersecurity enthusiasts.

Ways to contribute include:

- Developing educational games.
- Creating lesson plans and worksheets.
- Improving existing learning materials.
- Reviewing technical accuracy.
- Suggesting new educational topics.
- Improving accessibility and documentation.

Please read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) before submitting a contribution.

All contributions should be educational, age-appropriate, and focused on ethical technology use.

## 🔐 Our Approach to Cybersecurity

All technical activities and simulations are designed for authorized, controlled, and educational environments.

We promote responsible disclosure, digital safety, ethical learning, and respect for privacy.
